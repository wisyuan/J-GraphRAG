\title{J-GraphRAG: Replacing All LLM Generation in GraphRAG Indexing\\
with Jacobian-Lens Workspace Readout}

\author{Anonymous}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Graph-based retrieval-augmented generation (GraphRAG) frameworks such as
LightRAG, HippoRAG2, and MS-GraphRAG build their knowledge graphs with LLM
\texttt{generate()} calls at every extraction step: 5--60 seconds per document,
structured-output parsing with retries, unreliable JSON on 7B-scale models, and
\$5--15 of API cost per thousand documents. We show that \emph{every} extraction
step can be replaced by a single-forward-pass readout of the model's internal
\emph{workspace} with the Jacobian Lens (J-Lens): 0.17\,s per chunk, no
structured output, zero API cost. A hybrid extraction architecture---J-Lens
readout for the abstract layer (concepts, relations, roles, disambiguation),
text-side rules for the surface layer (named entities), vector geometry for
attributes, and graph/tensor methods for multi-hop reasoning---retains
\textbf{121\%/123\%} (LightRAG-J) and \textbf{125\%/95\%} (HippoRAG-J) of the
original pipelines' accuracy on GraphRAG-Bench (medical/novel). We further show
that the extracted artifacts are mathematically closed: concept vectors are
grounded against the symbolic relation structure (matching bge-m3 after
debiasing), and all graph retrieval operations linearize exactly into matrix
multiplication.
\end{abstract}
